要做到DevOps自動部署，一定要先學會如何手動部署到K8s，[kubectl](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/kubectl/)是官方出的cli工具，透過kubectl可以完全操作K8s叢集。

* * *

#### 常見指令

Kubernetes是由各種[resource](https://kubernetes.io/docs/reference/kubectl/#resource-types)組成，包含容器架構調度，以下說明一些常用到kubectl 指令。

*   查看常用的Resource：`kubectl get all`

![](/images/medium/6d15b538765caa1a.png)

*   查看所有節點：`kubectl get node`

![](/images/medium/b4b09deba11b1cab.png)

*   查看Pod資訊：`kubectl describe pod <pod_name>`

![](/images/medium/2f9a2e2f5e2d78c8.png)

*   查看Pod log：`kubectl logs <pod_name>`

![](/images/medium/b84e0e49628c035e.png)

*   進入Pod：`kubectl exe -it <pod_name> -- /bin/bash`

![](/images/medium/72d83f09f13b8c3a.png)

*   部署、更新resource：`kubectl apply -f <file_name>.yml`

![](/images/medium/2b2c52fc7ff94a2d.png)

* * *

#### kubectl apply

我們模擬上一篇的作法，將NGINX部署為deployment，並在ingress使用Google簽署的憑證，我們先在Cloud Shell建立一個nginx.yml

```yaml
apiVersion: apps/v1
kind: Deployment
metadata:
  name: deployment-nginx-3
spec:
  replicas: 1
  selector:
    matchLabels:
      app: pod-nginx-3
  template:
    metadata:
      labels:
        app: pod-nginx-3
    spec:
      containers:
      - name: nginx
        image: nginx:1.23
        ports:
        - containerPort: 80

---
apiVersion: v1
kind: Service
metadata:
  name: service-nginx-3
spec:
  type: ClusterIP
  ports:
  - port: 80
    protocol: TCP
    targetPort: 80
  selector:
    app: pod-nginx-3

---
apiVersion: networking.gke.io/v1
kind: ManagedCertificate
metadata:
  name: cert-nginx-3
spec:
  domains:
  - nginx-3.twcanhelp.me

---
apiVersion: networking.k8s.io/v1
kind: Ingress
metadata:
  name: ingress-nginx-3
  annotations:
    networking.gke.io/managed-certificates: cert-nginx-3
spec:
  rules:
  - host: nginx-3.twcanhelp.me
    http:
      paths:
      - path: /
        pathType: Prefix
        backend:
          service:
            name: service-nginx-3
            port:
              number: 80
```

接下來下`kubectl apply -f nginx.yml`，成功部署會顯示如下

![](/images/medium/31e8f6a48bcb60d7.png)

這時候看看ingress是否有能到Publci IP，用`kubectl get ingress`，剛剛部署的ingress-nginx-3還沒拿到，需要再等一下。

![](/images/medium/40c9b2a873d92066.png)

等了幾分鐘後，就會出現IP了。

![](/images/medium/73f6e4205ac7dd0e.png)

接著就去上一筆DNS的A紀錄，我是用Cloudflare管DNS，由於我們用Google幫我們簽的憑證，不要開Proxy

![](/images/medium/322bb6ff8c3c17b6.png)

再來就可以訪問看看網址，Google簽署憑證會需要時間，大概快十分鐘，過一段時間在看看網站就好了。
